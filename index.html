Questions worth answering early

Before the first frame moves.

Is Lumen Drift only a focus timer? +

No. The timer is the entry point. The planned product also supports reflection, mood tracking, saved streaks, and daily settings that help a person build a more intentional practice.

Why does the motion need to be built, not simply demonstrated? +

The breathing orb, completion burst, and comet are not decoration. They mark progress and completion. Building them with Reanimated lets the app respond naturally across the full session rather than presenting a fixed illustration.

Which states are included beyond the ideal flow? +

The planned scope accounts for splash loading and error states, onboarding, empty states, session states, sign-in, and settings. These are product screens, not afterthoughts.

How should the Home, Sky, and Done visuals be supplied? +

Each should be prepared as its own source visual: the Home orb, the Sky comet, and the Done particle burst. That gives the motion system clean assets to animate rather than forcing one phone composition into three unrelated moments.
